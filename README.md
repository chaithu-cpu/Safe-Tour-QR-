📌 About Our Project

Imagine being at a temple, festival, or tourist place and suddenly getting separated from your family. In a crowded place, finding your loved ones can be difficult and stressful.

SAFE TOUR QR is our attempt to solve this problem using technology.

Our project uses QR codes to connect registrations with relevant information. It also provides a way to report missing-person cases and allows authorized officials to verify details and manage reports.

Our main aim is simple: make identification easier and help families reunite safely.

❓ Why Do We Need This?

In crowded places, people can easily get separated from their families, especially children and elderly people.

Some common problems are:

- 🔎 Finding a missing person can take time.
- 📢 Manual announcements may not always be effective.
- 📋 Managing missing-person reports on paper can be difficult.
- 🔐 Sharing personal information publicly can create privacy risks.

We wanted to explore a simple, technology-based approach to these challenges.

💡 What Does SAFE TOUR QR Do?

Here are the main features of our project:

- 👤 User Registration — Register individuals through the system.
- 📱 QR Code Generation — Generate a unique QR code for each registration.
- 🚨 Missing-Person Reporting — Submit reports when someone goes missing.
- 🛡️ Official Verification — Allow authorized officials to verify information.
- 📂 Case Management — Help organize and track missing-person reports.
- 🔒 Privacy Protection — Restrict access to sensitive information.

Note: The availability of each feature depends on the current implementation of our website.

A project designed to help people should also protect them.

We aim to keep sensitive information restricted to authorized personnel and avoid exposing private details through publicly accessible QR codes.

🌟 About the Project

SAFE TOUR QR is a smart digital solution designed to help identify missing persons and support family reunification in crowded locations such as temples, festivals, tourist destinations, and public events.

The system uses QR-code-based identification to connect registered individuals with relevant information. It also provides a process for reporting missing-person cases and enables authorized officials to verify details and manage cases.

Our goal is to make missing-person identification more organized, efficient, and privacy-conscious.

🚨 Problem Statement

In crowded public places, children, elderly people, and other visitors can become separated from their families.

Traditional identification and manual reporting methods can create several challenges:

- 🔴 Delayed Identification: Manual announcements and paper records can slow down the identification process.
- 🔴 Family Separation: Finding family members in crowded locations can be difficult.
- 🔴 Information Management: Handling missing-person reports manually can make case tracking challenging.
- 🔴 Privacy Risks: Publicly exposing sensitive personal information can put vulnerable individuals at risk.

💡 Our Proposed Solution

SAFE TOUR QR proposes a QR-based system that combines public registration, QR-code generation, missing-person reporting, and authorized verification.

✨ Our Approach

- 📱 Register individuals through the system.
- 🔳 Generate a unique QR code for each registration.
- 📝 Allow users to report missing-person cases.
- 🛡️ Enable authorized officials to verify information.
- 🔍 Support case matching and tracking.
- ❤️ Help facilitate family reunification while protecting sensitive information.

⚙️ How It Works

The proposed workflow follows six simple steps:

01 → Register
A public user registers through the system.

02 → Generate QR Code
A unique QR code is issued for the registration.

03 → Report a Missing Person
A missing-person case is submitted through the system.

04 → Verify Information
Authorized officials check the submitted details.

05 → Match the Case
Relevant reports and available records are reviewed to identify a possible match.

06 → Reunite & Close the Case
After the case is appropriately verified and resolved, it can be marked as closed.

🔐 Privacy & Security

Protecting personal information is an important part of SAFE TOUR QR.

Our design priorities include:

- Restricting sensitive records to authorized personnel.
- Using appropriate access controls for administrative functions.
- Avoiding unnecessary public disclosure of personal information.
- Protecting registration and missing-person data.
- Ensuring responsible handling of information about children and vulnerable individuals.

Important: QR codes should not expose sensitive personal details directly. Appropriate authorization, consent, and verification procedures are essential.
